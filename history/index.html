Still open

  • VIN

    Not on file. Needed before title, insurance, or a serious M Edition claim.

  • Is it really an M Edition?

    Factory M Edition for 1995 was Merlot Mica. This car is Classic Red now. Treat M Edition as unverified until the VIN and a cowl/tag check say otherwise.

  • E85 vs pump gas

    AEM 50-1200 is an E85 pump. That does not mean the tank is E85. Confirm fuel before any Auto Sauce tune.

  • 800 / 350 vs a later spring swap

    Red Co sheet is 800 F / 350 R. Sale sheet was 700 / 325. No receipt for a later swap. Run the sheet numbers until someone measures the springs.
